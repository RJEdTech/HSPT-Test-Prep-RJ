Build your vocabulary

Nearly half of Verbal Skills comes down to whether you know the word. These are the 131 words this site's own questions test, plus a few more at the same level, so learning them here pays off directly in the practice sets.

How to use these. Look at the word and try to say what it means before you flip the card. The guessing is what makes it stick — a card you flip straight away teaches you almost nothing. Ten words a day beats 131 words the night before.

Every word, its meaning and a sentence that shows it in use. Use your browser's find (Ctrl-F, or Command-F on a Mac) to jump to one.

Once a set of words feels familiar, go and use them: synonym practice, antonym practice or vocabulary in context. Recognising a word on a flashcard and picking it out of four choices under time are different skills, and the second one is the one the test asks for.
