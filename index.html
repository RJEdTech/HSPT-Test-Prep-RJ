What's on the HSPT

Five sections, 298 questions, two hours and twenty-one minutes. Every question is multiple choice, and there is no penalty for a wrong answer — which is the single most useful fact on this page. Never leave one blank.

Sixteen seconds a question in Verbal Skills is the number that surprises people. That section is not really testing how much time you spend thinking — it is testing whether you know the word. The section guide goes through what each one is actually asking for, and the main page has the same table alongside the test dates.

How to use this site

Four steps, in this order. Find your starting point first — it is 40 questions and it ends by naming the skills you are weakest at, with a link straight to each one. Read the lesson for each of those two or three skills, then drill them until they stop feeling hard. Then sit the timed practice test near the end, because knowing an answer and knowing it in 16 seconds are different things.

Then go round again. The retake is where the score moves — the first pass only tells you where to aim.

If you have two days rather than two months, find your starting point and read the section guide. Knowing the format is most of the benefit.

The study plans on the main page put dates on all of that — six weeks, three weeks or one week, day by day.

One honest word about the test. Regis Jesuit reads your HSPT score alongside your grades, your teacher recommendations, your activities and your interview. It is one of several things we look at. Students who prepare a little tend to do better than students who panic a lot.

For parents

This is a free study site run by Regis Jesuit High School. There is nothing to buy, no account to create and no email address to give. Your student's answers are stored in this browser only — they are never sent to the school or to anyone else, and clearing the browser's site data erases them.

Anyone preparing for the HSPT is welcome to use it; your student does not need to be applying to Regis Jesuit. Test dates, registration, fee waivers and testing accommodations are handled by the Admissions Welcome Center — admissions@regisjesuit.com or 303.269.8000.

The most useful thing you can do is help your student pick a study plan and keep to it. Short sessions spread over weeks beat one long night, by a wide margin.
