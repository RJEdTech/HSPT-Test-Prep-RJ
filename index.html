What's on the HSPT

Five sections, 298 questions, two hours and twenty-one minutes. Every question is multiple choice, and there is no penalty for a wrong answer — which is the single most useful fact on this page. Never leave one blank.

Sixteen seconds a question in Verbal Skills is the number that surprises people. That section is not really testing how much time you spend thinking — it is testing whether you know the word. The section guide goes through what each one is actually asking for.

How to use this site

If you have a few weeks, the order that works is: read the guide, take the diagnostic to find out where you actually stand, drill the two or three weakest skills, then sit the practice test near the end so you find out how you handle the clock.

If you have two days, read the guide and take the practice test. Knowing the format is most of the benefit.

One honest word about the test. Regis Jesuit reads your HSPT score alongside your grades, your teacher recommendations, your activities, and your interview. It is one of several things we look at. Students who prepare a little tend to do better than students who panic a lot.
