How to use this site

  1. Find your starting point. It ends by naming the skills you are weakest at, with a link straight to each one.
  2. Read the lesson for each of those two or three skills.
  3. Drill them until they stop feeling hard.
  4. Take the timed practice test. Knowing an answer and knowing it in 16 seconds are different things.

Then go through it again. The second pass is where the score moves; the first only tells you where to aim. Your study plan puts all of it on real dates, counted back from December 5, and rebuilds itself every time you find your starting point again.

Two days rather than two months? Find your starting point and read the section guide. Knowing the format is most of the benefit.

One honest word about the test. Regis Jesuit reads your HSPT score alongside your grades, your teacher recommendations, your activities and your interview. It is one of several things we look at. Students who prepare a little tend to do better than students who panic a lot.
