This is a full-length form. Five sections, 298 questions, 2 hours 21 minutes — the real test's length, section sizes and per-section timing. Questions are drawn fresh from the bank each time, so a second sitting is a different test.

It is practice, not the exam. Every question here was written for this site to resemble the HSPT in format and difficulty. It is not a copy of any real exam and no real exam questions were used. The test you sit in December may prove harder or easier than this, and a score here does not predict a score there. What it does tell you is which skills to work on.

Before you start

  • Find two and a half uninterrupted hours. Stopping halfway defeats the point.
  • Phone away. Paper and pencil next to you for the math.
  • Each section is timed on its own. When time runs out, it moves on — the same as the real test.
  • No feedback until the end. You will see everything you missed once you finish.
  • No penalty for wrong answers here either. Guess rather than leave a blank.
