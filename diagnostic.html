Do not worry about the overall score. The number is not the point; the breakdown is. A student who finds out in September that analogies are their weak spot has three months to fix it.

Timing is where most students actually lose points, so the clock is on by default. If you would rather find out what you know first, switch it off — just take it timed at least once before test day.
