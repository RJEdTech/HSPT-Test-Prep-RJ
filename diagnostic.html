Where do you actually stand?

Forty questions, drawn from all five sections. At the end you get a breakdown by skill and a short list of what to work on first.

Work straight through without looking anything up — a diagnostic is only useful if it is honest. You will not see whether you got each one right until the end, which is also how the real test works. Expect about 25 minutes.

Do not worry about your score. The number is not the point; the breakdown is. A student who finds out in September that analogies are their weak spot has three months to fix it.
